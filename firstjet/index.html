Terms you are learning

The model is also a finance and aviation lesson.

Load factor

The percentage of available seats occupied by passengers. Higher load factor usually spreads fixed costs across more passengers.

Ancillary revenue

Revenue beyond the base fare—bags, seats, onboard products, and other passenger fees.

Contribution

The money left after the model subtracts operating costs. It is not the same as net income or cash flow.

This is an independent educational model based on public information, comparable-airline benchmarks, and explicit assumptions. It is not First Jet Airways' internal model, financial advice, or a forecast of actual results.
