The starting point

What if learning finance felt more like discovering something?

Many of us entered adulthood without a clear explanation of credit, debt, saving, investing, taxes, or retirement accounts.

This site is being built to make those ideas easier to approach: plain language, visible tradeoffs, and no assumption that you already know the vocabulary.
